# NoteForge demo video script

Target length: about 90 seconds. Record at 1280x800 or 1920x1080.
No face circle and no photo overlay. Professional edit: clean cuts,
visible cursor with click ripple, short feature labels, zoomed text
so it reads on a phone, narration cleaned and levelled.

## Script (timed)

**[0:00 - 0:08] Introduction (title card, then app on screen)**
"Hello, I am Ibrahim, a student at FUT Minna. This is NoteForge. It turns your lecture notes into flashcards, a quiz and a topic map, using machine learning that runs right in your browser."

**[0:08 - 0:25] The problem and the forge**
"Re-reading notes is a weak way to study. Watch: I load my Biology notes and press Forge. In one second NoteForge reads 204 words, picks the key terms with TF-IDF, and writes a three-sentence summary."

**[0:25 - 0:42] Flashcards**
"Here are the flashcards, built from my own sentences. Fill the gap, or define the term. I flip the card, test myself, and mark what I know. The progress bar shows how much of the topic I have covered."

**[0:42 - 0:58] Quiz**
"Now the quiz. Every question comes from the notes themselves, and the wrong options are other key terms from the same notes, so I cannot guess my way through. Instant feedback, and a score at the end."

**[0:58 - 1:12] Topic Map**
"The Topic Map shows the machine learning at work. A naive Bayes classifier scores my notes against eight subjects: Biology at one hundred percent. Below, the TF-IDF key terms, sized by weight."

**[1:12 - 1:22] Library and offline**
"I save the set to my library. Everything lives on this device, no account and no signal needed. It works fully offline."

**[1:22 - 1:30] Closing (hold final frame, gentle fade)**
"NoteForge: paste your notes, and forge them into study tools. Try the live demo, the link is in the description. Thank you."

## Recording notes

- Start on the title card for 2 seconds, app visible by 0:03, first result on screen by 0:20.
- Use the Biology sample for the main flow; it forges 11 flashcards and 8 quiz questions.
- Keep the cursor visible with a click ripple on every click; zoom so card and quiz text reads on a phone.
- Change the screen every 5 to 10 seconds; cut cleanly between scenes; never speed up the voice.
- Feature labels, short, top corner: "TF-IDF key terms", "Flashcards", "Generated quiz", "Naive Bayes topic map", "Offline library".
- Voice chain: denoise lightly, high-pass, gentle compression, loudness to about -14 LUFS, true peak at or below -1 dB.
- Thumbnail (no photo): dark navy background, huge bold text "NOTES TO QUIZ!" with "NoteForge" badge, orange accent, readable at small size.
- End on the study pack screen with the final line held 1 to 2 seconds, then a gentle fade.
